Zero-G Academy

Learn real space science by floating through it. Missions, quizzes and simulations for curious cadets aged 7 to 12.

About Us

What we teach

Short lessons about the people who fly in space and the spaceships that carry them, with a quiz and a simulation for every lesson.

How it works

Enter your cadet name and take a five-question placement test. Your score sets your starting level, then you follow the lesson path one node at a time.

Real space numbers

Placement questions use values from NASA's Planetary Fact Sheet, so every fact you learn is one scientists actually use.
